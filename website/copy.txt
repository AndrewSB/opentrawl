[hero.sub]
OpenTrawl is a macOS app and a command line tool. It builds you a small index of all the data in your apps (notes, messages, e-mails). 
You can search it, and so can your (local) AI.

[hero.privacy]
Nothing leaves your machine, unless you want it to.

[sources.today]
**Available today:** iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Notes, Contacts, Calendar.

[sources.soon]
**Coming soon:** Gmail, Twitter, Photos.

[terminal.caption]
I took my real archive and told Claude to anonymise it by LARPing as The
Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. The data is real, the names aren't.

[fact.1.title]
Private by design

[fact.1.body]
Your archives are just files on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, we don't know who you are.
If you want to point a (local) AI at your archives, *you're* in control - not a company.

[fact.2.title]
It just works

[fact.2.body]
Install the Mac app. It builds search indexes, updates them, and updates the app for you.
No npm install hell, no MCP servers, no updates breaking everything.

[fact.3.title]
Open source

[fact.3.body]
The code is on GitHub, MIT licensed. Take a look, see how it works. No secrets here.

[fact.4.title]
Plugins - coming soon

[fact.4.body]
Want to search something that we haven't built yet? We'll share a skill for your coding agent - so you can write a plugin to search anything you like.
