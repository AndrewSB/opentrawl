Your AI can't search your iMessage.
OpenTrawl fixes this.

OpenTrawl is a command line tool that keeps a searchable copy of your app history on your Mac. You search it from the terminal, and your AI can search it the same way.

Nothing leaves your machine, unless you want it to.

Available today: iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Notes, Contacts, Calendar.
Coming soon: Gmail, Twitter, Photos.

Facts — option A

  

    
Private by design
    Your archives are files on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded and there is
    no account. If you point an AI at them, you choose which one, and what it sees.

    
It just works
    Install the Mac app. It builds the archive, keeps it fresh, and updates
    itself. No npm install hell, no config files, no scripts.

    
Open source
    The code is on GitHub. Read it yourself, or have your AI read it and
    tell you what it does.

    
Coming soon
    A skill that writes a crawler for any app you want to archive.
